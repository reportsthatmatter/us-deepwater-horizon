# Deep Water: The Gulf Oil Disaster and the Future of Offshore Drilling

Report to the President by the National Commission on the BP Deepwater Horizon
Oil Spill and Offshore Drilling, January 2011.

## Source

`archive/deep-water.pdf` — the GPO edition, `GPO-OILCOMMISSION`, published at
<https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GPO-OILCOMMISSION>. Public domain (work of
the U.S. Government). See `datapackage.json`.
